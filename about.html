About This Project

A student-led project focused on pediatric burn recovery: what happens after the initial injury, after the hospital, and throughout the months and years of healing that follow.

Beyond the Burn is a student-led project about pediatric burn recovery, especially the parts that happen after leaving the hospital. Recovery doesn't always end when the wound heals, and for many children and families, it can continue for months, years, or even a lifetime.

I started Beyond the Burn because of my own experience with a severe burn that affected my entire right leg when I was younger. I spent time in the hospital and continued returning for follow-up appointments and scar treatments for years afterward. I was fortunate to recover well, but looking back, I realize there were parts of recovery that were difficult to navigate as a child, especially learning to feel comfortable with my scars and dealing with how others sometimes reacted to them.

Over time, I began wondering what recovery looked like for other children and their families. Everyone's experience is different, but I think there's a lot we can learn from hearing each other's stories. I wanted to create a space where people could share their experiences, better understand the recovery process, and find resources that might be helpful along the way.

Through Beyond the Burn, I hope to bring together survivor stories, reliable information, and insights from families and healthcare professionals. My goal is simply to make the recovery process a little easier to understand and a little less lonely.

Founder’s Note

This project is still growing. My hope is to build it slowly and thoughtfully, learning from burn survivors, families, clinicians, and organizations already doing important work in this space.
